# Artwork

This public template includes only generic botanical SVG placeholders and icons created for the template. The sample names Partner One and Partner Two, date and venue text are fictional placeholders. No real wedding photographs or original couple illustrations are included.

Replace all five story frames and the venue image with your own artwork, keeping rights to any images you use. Source SVG placeholders and icons are covered by the repository’s MIT licence.

No guest photos, guest lists, RSVP records, owner credentials or private files are included.
