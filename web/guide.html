A little help.

Reply and share photos

Open the personal invitation link sent by the hosts. You can edit your reply using the same link. Keep that link private: it gives access to your invitation’s reply and photo allowance.

Original-quality photos

Choose photos from your phone, or use its camera. The app uploads the file supplied by your browser without resizing or recompressing it. Some phones convert photos in their photo picker; choose original files where your phone offers that option.

Keep the page open during uploads. If the connection drops, retry. After reopening the same tab, select the same files to resume while the upload session remains active. Photos stay private until the hosts approve them.

Add to your home screen

On Android, use the browser’s Install app or Add to home screen option. On iPhone, open in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Installation is optional.

The invitation and its artwork can be available offline after the initial cache finishes. RSVP and photo uploads require internet. If the installed app asks for your invitation again, paste the original personal link.

Shared devices

Use “Forget my invitation on this device” when finished. This clears this tab’s saved invitation and draft. Files you already uploaded remain with the hosts.
